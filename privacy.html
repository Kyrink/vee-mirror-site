Vee Mirror Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 1, 2026

Introduction

Vee Mirror is a personal smart-mirror wardrobe and styling assistant. This Privacy Policy describes how information may be used when an authorized user connects supported services to Vee Mirror.

Pinterest integration

Vee Mirror may allow an authorized user to connect their Pinterest account to access fashion inspiration saved to their own Pinterest account.

The application may request permission to read:

  • The authenticated user's Pinterest boards
  • The authenticated user's saved Pins
  • Information associated with those Pins and boards that Pinterest makes available through its API

How Pinterest data is used

Pinterest data may be used to:

  • Display saved fashion inspiration
  • Understand the user's style preferences
  • Identify preferred colors, silhouettes, garments, and aesthetics
  • Improve personalized outfit recommendations

Data practices

  • Vee Mirror does not sell Pinterest data.
  • Vee Mirror does not use Pinterest data for advertising.
  • Vee Mirror does not use the Pinterest integration to manage advertising campaigns.
  • Vee Mirror does not intentionally use the integration to access Pins or boards belonging to other Pinterest users.
  • Pinterest information is used to provide personal wardrobe and styling functionality.

Data storage

Information derived from the user's saved fashion inspiration may be stored by Vee Mirror for the purpose of providing personalized wardrobe and styling recommendations.

Authorization

Pinterest access is limited to an account that has explicitly authorized Vee Mirror.

Revoking access

The user may revoke Vee Mirror's Pinterest access through their Pinterest account settings.

Data deletion

Users may request deletion of Pinterest-derived information stored by Vee Mirror by contacting the application owner.

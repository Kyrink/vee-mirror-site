VEE MIRROR

Your wardrobe. Your style. Smarter.

Vee Mirror is a personal smart-mirror wardrobe assistant designed to help users organize their closet, create outfits, and receive personalized styling recommendations.

About

Vee Mirror combines wardrobe organization, fashion inspiration, and personalized styling to help the user make better use of clothing already in their wardrobe.

Fashion inspiration

Vee Mirror can connect to a user's own fashion inspiration sources, including saved Pinterest boards and Pins, to better understand their personal style preferences.

Saved inspiration may be used to identify styling preferences such as colors, silhouettes, textures, footwear, and outfit combinations.

Vee Mirror can then use those preferences to help create outfit recommendations using clothing already available in the user's wardrobe.

Project status

Vee Mirror is currently a private personal project and is not available as a public consumer service.
